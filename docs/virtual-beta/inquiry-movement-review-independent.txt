Post hoc independent movement review — 2026-11-05

Finding
The eight runs support concrete concerns about repeated home/account movement, but do not establish that either actor is humanlike or that aligned-v1 is less humanlike. The existing 3/4 versus 1/4 goal outcome is not a naturalness measure. Goal failure, leaving without using a particular button, and revisiting a previously unhelpful place are not by themselves behavioral defects. This review is post hoc; it introduces no score, numeric revisit rule, or blanket revisit ban.

Evidence scope and checks
Only the eight fictional trials in reports/inquiry-alignment-2026-11-05/live, current related source and the current inquiry-alignment report were read. No API, .env, historical participant payload, product/docs/Git edit, or new run was used. Six primary input PNGs were visually inspected. All 88 current PNG hashes match their recorded delivered hashes. All 72 opportunities to carry a previous admitted inquiry outcome into the next assessment do so, and the same earlier result is also in the current action memory. This establishes delivery, not comprehension. Expectation arrays are empty throughout; working conditions and other model recollections still change. Full paths, SHA values, distributions, weights, available conditions, memory channels and reviewer-only state are in movement-evidence.json.

Home at 9 and 11 is exactly the same PNG (c298dae5…); account at 10 and 12 is exactly the original account PNG from step 3 (6d04d9f6…). At 9/11 raw and final are e2 (account), with raw probability .90/.62. At account 10 raw e1 (confirm)=.96 versus e2 (home)=.04, but weight .01 on e1 makes final e2. At account 12 raw e1=.56 versus e2=.40, again final e2 after e1 weight .01. Hence repeated unchanged home→account→home movement was executed even while the raw actor preferred performing confirmation. All four actions were UI_PERFORMED. Reviewer-only snapshots show verificationAttempts=1 and recordChecks=0, unchanged; those hidden counters were not actor knowledge.
The policy is released at home because all three plausible clicks are deferred (ALTERNATIVES_EXHAUSTED), while account still applies DEFER_WITH_ALTERNATIVES only to confirmation. This asymmetry concretely explains the final bounce. It does not prove what an unweighted actor would do on subsequent steps: its later input would differ.
The initial receipt PNG at step 4 visibly says R-001 was accepted but record reconciliation remains incomplete. That transient receipt is absent at 10/12. It is not correct to infer total forgetting: the delivered continuity and working-condition memory still contains o4-based “予約記録との照合がまだ完了していません。” at 12; step 10 experience also retains the performed o3/e1→o4 result, classified conflicts/not_supported. Retrying confirmation could be plausible uncertainty caused by the returned account page and renewed “利用情報の確認が必要” warning; the trace alone cannot certify human plausibility or identify forgetting.

Account step 10 applies weight .01 to e1, but does not change the winning choice; at 12 all weights are one (ALTERNATIVES_EXHAUSTED). Thus this tail is not explained away by final-choice overrides. At 10–12 the model repeatedly chooses current_c3/test_condition/condition_status_known about the saved afternoon session, receives condition_uncertain/current_image, and repeats navigation without new pixels or a supported condition change. The previous outcomes reach the next input; account/home observations are in the immediate image memory. An o4-based recollection that reconciliation remains incomplete also persists. At 12 the effect prediction for leaving to the same home changes to advances, citing the previous home o11, despite no newly displayed state in the cycle. That is a specific weakly supported expectation, and the unchanged checking cycle is the strongest model-level concern here. It still does not establish that such short confused exploration is impossible or atypical for humans; no matched human trials are available.
Earlier workshop r1 step 7 raw e2=.94 became final e3=.01 after e1/e2 weights .01. This exposed records before the tail. The same wrapper therefore sometimes interrupts account revisits rather than always creating them.

Case 4 — legacy workshop r2, steps 5–12: exploration and an override can both be plausible
After the initial receipt, step 5 raw/final e1=.54 returns to the blocked change page (step 6), then step 7 e2=.89 revisits account. Step 8 raw confirm e1=.99 becomes home e2=.01 under e1 weight .01. At home 9 raw account e2=.74 becomes records e3=.08 under e1/e2 weights .01. This changes the branch to a previously unvisited records screen, not automatically an abnormal action. The actor leaves records at 10 (raw/final e2=.91), returns to account via home 11 (.86), and at 12 raw/final e1=.90 performs confirmation after weights release. Terminal audit records two verification attempts; no subsequent decision is recorded.
A person may inspect an alternative, not find the expected option, and revisit the earlier confirmation page. This whole unsuccessful run is compatible with that explanation. The data do not justify marking it unnatural merely because records' reconciliation control was not used or the goal remained unmet. Its two overrides also show why “override” alone is not a defect label.

Case 5 — aligned library r1, steps 4–9: unsuccessful revisiting can be information-driven
Step 4 visibly adds the receipt/reconciliation message and raw/final e2=.72 returns home. Step 5 raw/final e3=.66 opens records, which visibly shows saved Central Library, R-001, and the reconciliation control. At 6 raw/final e2=.56 leaves records; at 7 raw/final e1=.92 tries the change page again, where 8 shows the same blocked PNG as 1. Step 8 e1=.96 returns home; 9 e2=.90 revisits account. Weights do not alter these choices. The actor has encountered different information between visits, including a receipt, a records page, and renewed blocking. Expecting the change page to have become available after confirmation, then returning to the named prerequisite when it has not, is a plausible exploratory interpretation. Not pressing reconcile at 6 is not independently evidence of unnatural movement. This early exploration should be distinguished from the later repeated empty exits in Case 1.

Across the eight runs
There are seven final selections different from raw model choice: aligned library r1 10/12, aligned workshop r1 7, aligned workshop r2 10/12, and legacy workshop r2 8/9. All are produced by the shared temporary-deferral policy, not aligned-v1-specific weight changes. Four account exits are necessary parts of the observed aligned library r1/workshop r2 bounces; two home overrides in workshop runs expose records and interrupt a proposed return to account. The remaining legacy account exit likewise comes from shared weights. These are local selection explanations, not counterfactual whole-run outcomes.
The other four runs (legacy library r1/r2, legacy workshop r1, aligned library r2) show the visible receipt→records→reconciliation-feedback→change-page sequence. Revisiting the same home PNG after new feedback is ordinary transit and carries changed available information. Their goal attainment does not validate human fidelity any more than the unsuccessful runs invalidate it. DECISION_LIMIT is a system boundary, not an observed human decision to stop.

Relevant current-code facts
app/RecoveryFixture.tsx:10 clears feedback on route changes; :14 confirmation increments attempts and displays the receipt; :15 reconciliation changes eligibility. Hidden state must not be imputed to the actor. runner/virtual-beta-core.ts:82 builds an allowlisted model input and :155–172 attaches bounded memory/reason images; :228 projects admitted outcome memory into the action input; :252–266 applies shared weights after the raw response. jev/virtual-beta/experience.ts:82 explicitly says a return is not a failure; the experience archive is bounded separately from image memory. jev/virtual-beta/selection.ts:45–73 and :98–116 apply the UI-conditional policy. These facts explain the delivered behavior; they do not supply a human naturalness ground truth.

No goal-optimization change is proposed. The warranted correction is to separate observed repeated movement, plausible exploration, available memory, fallible model expectations, and wrapper-induced final choices before drawing conclusions about naturalness. Neither old nor new can be certified humanlike from these data.
